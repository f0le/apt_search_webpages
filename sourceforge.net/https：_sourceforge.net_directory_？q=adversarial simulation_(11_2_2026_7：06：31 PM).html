    pix2pixHD

    Synthesizing and manipulating 2048x1024 images with conditional GANs

    pix2pixHD is a PyTorch-based implementation of a conditional generative adversarial network designed for high-resolution image-to-image translation, capable of producing photorealistic outputs at resolutions up to 2048×1024. It is widely used to convert structured inputs such as semantic label maps into realistic images, making it particularly valuable in applications like autonomous driving simulation, face synthesis, and scene generation.
